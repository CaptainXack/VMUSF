# VMUSF policy and case routing model

VMUSF is resolution-first. The assistant explains the process, but verified rules determine which actions and escalations are available.

## Universal case rules

1. Classify the issue before recommending a route.
2. Keep community/forum access available at all times. Leaving a case never loses progress.
3. Use repeatable gated sections. A section may contain multiple calls, chats, appointments, documents and promises.
4. Every section explains What, Why, When, Where, Who, information required, choices, evidence, and what happens next.
5. The final action is Close this section. A section cannot close until required evidence exists or an explicit reason for unavailable information is recorded.
6. AI-extracted facts from calls/documents are suggestions until confirmed by the member.
7. Official/provider, regulatory, verified VMUSF and community evidence remain visibly separate.
8. Apply current rules by effective date. Newer regulator rules override stale provider guidance where they conflict.
9. Do not invent legal/regulatory eligibility or compensation amounts. Retrieve current verified material when needed.
10. Resolved cases enter monitoring before closure. After verified local archive, personal server-side case data can be purged according to the privacy/cost design.

## Issue families and routes

### Broadband / Wi-Fi
Identify Hub/model, wired vs Wi-Fi scope, total loss vs slow/intermittent service, outage evidence, diagnostics, Virgin fault reporting, engineer actions and monitoring. Check automatic compensation only against the current scheme conditions.

### TV
Treat TV 360, Stream, V6 and TiVo separately. Capture box, error code, affected channels/apps/recordings/remote/audio/picture and network dependency. TV complaints can use Virgin's complaint/ADR process, but TV-only loss is not automatically a fixed broadband/home-phone compensation event.

### Landline / Digital Voice
Identify traditional calling symptoms vs Digital Voice setup and equipment. Capture total loss, accessibility/vulnerability impact, fault report and engineer outcome. Check current automatic-compensation rules where applicable.

### Billing, payments, credits and refunds
Capture exact bill, line item, amount, expected amount, payment evidence and previous credits. Resolve with Virgin first. If unresolved, formal complaint then current ADR/deadlock route when eligible. Financial-product complaints branch separately.

### Contracts, renewals and sales
Preserve pre-contract/point-of-sale information, term, promised price, later change, end date and early termination issue. Use current Ofcom contract protections and Virgin complaint process.

### Switching
For qualifying fixed residential broadband/landline switches, recognise One Touch Switch. Track gaining/losing provider, match/order references, date, downtime, post-switch charges and number-porting issues. Do not tell users to cancel old fixed service separately when OTS should manage the switch.

### Cancellation / termination
Record every cancellation attempt, channel, timestamp, confirmation/reference, contract status and final bill. Repeated contacts stay inside the same section until the outcome is known.

### Installation / engineer appointments
Capture booking date/window/reference, attendance, findings, work performed, replacement equipment and post-visit outcome. Allow repeated engineer visits. Check current automatic-compensation rules for qualifying delayed activation or missed appointments.

### Automatic compensation
Use current Virgin/Ofcom scheme material at decision time. Do not hard-code rates. Distinguish qualifying total loss/delayed activation/missed appointment from Wi-Fi-only, intermittent, slow or TV-only issues.

### Debt / financial difficulty
Prioritise support and proportionate treatment. Capture arrears/bill position, provider assistance, payment arrangements and any restriction/disconnection action. Do not demand unnecessary sensitive information. A dispute about treatment may become a complaint.

### Accessibility / vulnerability
This is cross-cutting. Preferred communication, accessible formats, authorised representative and needed adjustments should follow the member through every workflow.

### Subject Access Request (SAR)
Keep SAR as a separate data-rights track even when it arises from the same events as a service complaint. A SAR can be verbal or written; provider forms may be convenient but are not treated as the only possible format. Track valid receipt, identity/authority requests, clarification, statutory response deadline, any valid extension and response completeness.

### Other data rights
Track rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability separately. Check applicable conditions/exemptions before suggesting entitlement.

### Privacy / data-protection complaint
Record the underlying data issue, rights request(s), Virgin correspondence and final response. Give Virgin an opportunity to investigate before preparing an ICO complaint, while keeping rights-request clocks independent.

### Electronic marketing / PECR
Capture message/call channel, sender/number, timestamp, evidence and opt-out history. Keep this separate from ordinary customer-service complaints and from scam/fraud classification.

### Account security / fraud
Contain the security problem first. Never request passwords/security answers. Capture unauthorised changes, timestamps, account access, Virgin response and any downstream billing/privacy issue, then branch appropriately.

### Moving home
Track move date, old/new service dates, order/reference, equipment, installation commitments and billing. Split installation and billing failures into the relevant sub-route instead of one vague moving-home complaint.

### Bereavement / critical illness / authority to act
Use Virgin's dedicated routes and minimise repeated or unnecessary disclosure. Capture the desired outcome and only the authority documentation needed. Power of Attorney, Court of Protection, appointeeship and authorised-third-party cases remain private.

### Financial product / credit agreement
Do not route a regulated financial-product complaint to CISAS simply because Virgin is involved. Track complaint to the financial business, final response and Financial Ombudsman eligibility/time limits.

### Premium-rate / phone-paid services
Current regulation is Ofcom, not the former PSA. Identify the service/charge provider, challenge the relevant provider and use current Ofcom reporting guidance where appropriate. A Virgin billing error remains a billing route.

## Complaints and ADR

Virgin's published complaints process is the provider-level procedure for broadband, TV and phone service complaints. VMUSF records contact attempts, complaint references, proposed resolutions and manager/final-position outcomes. Telecom ADR eligibility uses the current Ofcom rule: six weeks from first formal complaint from 8 April 2026, or earlier where an appropriate deadlock route applies. Stale provider text referring to eight weeks must not override the newer Ofcom rule.

## Source precedence

For a regulated decision use, in order:
1. Current legislation/regulator/ombudsman guidance applicable to the issue.
2. Current Virgin Media terms, complaints code, policy or help material.
3. Verified VMUSF interpretation/workflow rule.
4. Confirmed community outcomes, labelled as community evidence.
5. Unverified community information only as a lead, never as official fact.

Every source-based rule stores authority, source, last-verified date and where useful an effective-from date. Changed sources are reviewable rather than silently rewriting active case history.
